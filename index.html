More try-ons with your Hugging Face account

The try-on demos run on Hugging Face's free GPUs. Without an account, everyone on your internet connection shares one small daily allowance. A free Hugging Face account gets its own, larger one.

  1. Sign up free at huggingface.co.
  2. Create a token at Settings → Access Tokens. Choose the Read type.
  3. Paste it here.

Your token stays in this browser. It goes only to Hugging Face and to its GPU demos (IDM-VTON, Leffa, FLUX) so they can count your allowance; never to Kolors or WeShop. You can delete it any time at huggingface.co.
